This zip file contains metadata and images of borehole samples (core, side wall core, cuttings etc) that are held at the National Core Store.
Images are low resolution and should only be used to assess completeness of sample, and suitability for in-person viewing and sub-sampling.
For more information about the National Core Store or to request a viewing, go to https://www.nzpam.govt.nz/maps-geoscience/core-store/
